Your build in Path of Building

Paste your PoB2 export code (Import/Export Build → Generate) or a pobb.in link. The app then judges every item with Path of Building's own calculations: what it does to your minion DPS, effective HP, resistances and Spirit. Make sure PoB's gear, tree, skills and Configuration match how you really play. Items you copy in game (F2) replace PoB's, so it always uses what you're wearing.

How the gear score works

Stat weights

Only used for By my weights searches, and for the verdict when the build is turned off. A weight says how many points one unit of a stat is worth: Life 1 and Fire Res 1.5 means +10% fire res = +15 life.

Settings

Hotkeys, the popup, startup and updates.

Language

Switching the interface language applies immediately and is saved.

Hotkeys

Hover an item in game and press the check-item hotkey. The app presses Ctrl+C for you (one keystroke per press). Pick a key the game doesn't use: an F-key like F2 works best. Letters such as D also reach the game (with WASD movement, D walks your character).

Startup

Trade site account (optional)

Sign in to pathofexile.com inside the app for Travel to hideout straight from the results window, and for fuller searches (the trade site allows bigger ones when you're signed in). Your password goes only to pathofexile.com; the app keeps the login cookie on this PC.

Updates

The installed app checks GitHub for a new version when it starts and every few hours, downloads it quietly, and installs it the next time you quit or restart.

Troubleshooting

If the test popup appears here but not in game, switch PoE2 to Windowed Fullscreen (Options → Graphics). If the hotkey does nothing, open the log. Your data is saved in %APPDATA%\poe2-upgrade-checker.
